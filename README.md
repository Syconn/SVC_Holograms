SVC Holograms
Bring immersive holographic communication to your Minecraft galaxy.

SVC Holograms adds support for the Holographic Communicators from Syconn's Star Wars Mod and integrates them with Simple Voice Chat, allowing players to communicate through live holographic projections.

When a call is placed, players can see and hear each other through holograms, recreating the iconic communication technology seen throughout the Star Wars universe.

Features
📡 Holographic Communicators from Syconn's Star Wars Mod
🎙️ Integration with Simple Voice Chat
👥 Live holographic player projections during calls
🌌 Immersive Star Wars-style communication
🔊 Real-time voice transmission through holograms
🛰️ Multiplayer call support

About
SVC Holograms is an addon that bridges the hologram technology from Syconn's Star Wars Mod with the functionality of Simple Voice Chat, creating a more immersive multiplayer experience.

Whether you're coordinating a mission across the galaxy or contacting allies from afar, holographic communication brings a new level of immersion to your Star Wars adventures.
